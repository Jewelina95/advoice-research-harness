# Search strategy

Date: 2026-09-23

## Research questions

1. What incremental role can a diagnostic Agent have beyond a supervised speech model?
2. What counts as explainability in a clinical Agent system?
3. Are evidence-chain replay, decision provenance, and reasoning-trace ideas present in prior work?
4. How does ADvoice differ from multimodal speech models, post-hoc XAI, concept bottlenecks, provenance standards, and clinical decision-support argumentation?

## Search themes

- Clinical agents: `clinical AI agent diagnosis sequential decision tool use audit trail`
- Explanation faithfulness: `chain of thought faithfulness causal intervention explanation`
- Clinical provenance: `clinical decision support provenance proof knowledge graph argumentation`
- Interpretable intermediate states: `concept bottleneck intervention clinical prediction`
- Speech cognitive screening XAI: `speech cognitive decline explainable AI systematic review`
- Reporting and evaluation: `TRIPOD+AI calibration clinical utility external validation`

## Inclusion priorities

1. Standards and reporting guidelines.
2. Peer-reviewed primary studies and systematic reviews.
3. Top-tier conference papers for concept bottlenecks and explanation faithfulness.
4. Preprints only when no peer-reviewed equivalent exists; these are labelled as preprints.

## Exclusion rules

- Free-form opinion without an executable or empirical method.
- Studies that call a generated rationale an explanation without testing its faithfulness.
- Results that compare different datasets, splits, encoders, or label mappings as if they measured Agent gain.

## Audit note

This is a focused narrative review for method design, not a PRISMA systematic review. It does not establish clinical effectiveness. Two iterative search cycles were used: first to map the conceptual landscape, then to target faithfulness, provenance, intervention, and matched incremental-value evaluation.
