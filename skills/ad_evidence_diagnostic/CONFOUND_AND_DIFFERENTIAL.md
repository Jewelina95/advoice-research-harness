# Confounds and alternative explanations

The Agent does not diagnose these conditions. It checks whether they limit interpretation or require clinical review.

## Acquisition and processing

- microphone/device, gain, compression, clipping, reverberation and background noise;
- recording duration, editing and public-video production;
- VAD/diarization error, interviewer leakage and task-boundary error;
- ASR deletion, insertion, repetition, language mismatch and punctuation artifacts.

## Task and interaction

- interviewer prompting, question complexity and response opportunity;
- unfamiliar picture/task, nonstandard instructions and incomplete task exposure;
- spontaneous speech versus constrained naming/fluency demands;
- longitudinal interval and practice effects.

## Person-level alternatives requiring clinical context

- hearing or vision impairment;
- depression, anxiety, fatigue, sleep problems or low engagement;
- education, literacy, multilingualism, dialect and cultural familiarity;
- dysarthria, aphasia, Parkinsonism, stroke or other neurological conditions;
- respiratory/voice disorders, pain and medication effects.

## Required action

When a major confound plausibly explains a key state, lower that state's reliability, cite the confound in `quality_evidence_ids`, preserve the alternative explanation in limitations, and prefer repeat standardized collection or formal assessment over stronger disease language.
